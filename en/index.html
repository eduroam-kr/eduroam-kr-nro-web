For research and education

Global Wi-Fi roaming,
eduroam

One account, free Wi-Fi at research and
education institutions worldwide

One account from your home institution connects you at participating sites worldwide. No guest account to apply for at each visit.

eduroam in the Republic of Korea is operated by KREONET, the national research network of KISTI, as the National Roaming Operator (NRO).

How to connect

  1. Check that your home institution participates. The list of institutions shows every one in Korea.
  2. Choose eduroam from the Wi-Fi list.
  3. Enter your home institution account as username@institution-domain.
